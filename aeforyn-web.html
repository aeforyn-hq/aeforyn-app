Recovery System
Step-by-step account recovery — guided, logged, and actionable
ℹ️
This playbook is active for the Instagram login alert detected 14 minutes ago. Work through each step in order. Your progress is saved and logged.
Instagram Recovery — Active
✓
Threat detected and evidence logged
Aeforyn detected the login at 09:27 from Lagos, Nigeria. IP, device fingerprint, and timestamp have been preserved in your incident log.
2
Change your Instagram password immediately
Go to Instagram → Settings → Security → Password. Use a new password of 16+ characters never used before. Do not use your name, birthday, or any variation of your current password.
3
Revoke all active sessions
Instagram → Settings → Security → Active Sessions → Log Out All Devices. This terminates the session the attacker currently holds.
4
Enable or reset 2FA with authenticator app
Do not use SMS 2FA — SIM swap attacks can bypass it. Use Google Authenticator or Authy. This is the single most effective protection you can add.
5
Review & remove third-party app access
Instagram → Settings → Security → Apps and Websites. Remove any app you don't recognise. Attackers use OAuth tokens to stay in even after a password change.
6
Download your incident report
Aeforyn has compiled your full incident log with timestamps, IPs, and actions taken. Download it for your records, your insurer, or any platform escalation.

How Content Storage Works
A plain-English explanation of how Aeforyn protects your creative work.
☁️ Where is my content stored?
Your files are stored on AWS S3 — the same cloud infrastructure used by Netflix, Airbnb, and thousands of major companies. Aeforyn purchases cloud storage wholesale from AWS and provides it to you through your subscription. You never deal with AWS directly.
🔒 Is it encrypted?
Yes. All files are encrypted with AES-256 before they leave your device and remain encrypted at rest. Even Aeforyn staff cannot view your content — only your account key can decrypt it.
💰 What does storage cost Aeforyn?
AWS S3 costs approximately $0.023/GB/month. A Pro Creator subscriber with 50GB costs Aeforyn roughly R21/month in storage. This is built into your subscription price — there are no separate storage fees.
🆘 What happens if I get hacked?
Nothing. Your vault is completely separate from your social accounts. A hacker who takes over your Instagram cannot touch your Aeforyn vault. You log in directly to Aeforyn and download everything you need to rebuild.
