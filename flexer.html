Recovery Playbook
Step-by-step guide if your account has been compromised
ℹ️
This playbook is pre-loaded for Instagram takeover recovery
Based on the active alert on your account. Work through each step in order. Do not skip steps.
Instagram Account Recovery — Active
✓
Threat Detected & Logged
Flexer detected the unrecognised login and logged all details including IP address, device fingerprint, and timestamp. Evidence is preserved.
2
Change Your Instagram Password Immediately
Go to Instagram Settings → Security → Password. Choose a new password you have never used anywhere. Use at minimum 16 characters with a mix of symbols and numbers. Do not reuse any previous password.
3
Revoke All Active Sessions
Instagram Settings → Security → Active Sessions → Log Out All Devices. This terminates any session the attacker may still have open, including the Lagos login.
4
Enable or Reset 2FA
Instagram Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication. If you already have 2FA, reset the authenticator app. Use an authenticator app (not SMS) — SIM swap attacks can bypass SMS 2FA.
5
Review & Revoke Third-Party App Access
Instagram Settings → Security → Apps and Websites. Remove any app you don't recognise or no longer use. Attackers often use OAuth tokens from third-party apps to maintain access even after a password change.
6
Report to Instagram & Document
Report the incident via Instagram's official reporting tool. Flexer has logged the full incident report — download it for your records and for any police or platform escalation if needed.
